about=FloodDepth turns a satellite image of a flood (or a flood-extent layer)
    and a DEM into a flood-depth map with uncertainty bands and a printable
    report. Every computation runs inside QGIS with NumPy and the GDAL
    bindings that QGIS ships: no external executables, no pip install.

    Find it under Raster > FloodDepth, on the Raster toolbar (one button) and
    in the Processing Toolbox. The user guide is installed with the plugin
    and opens from the menu and from every tool's Help button.

    Imagery: select the downloaded files as they are - Copernicus / EO
    Browser 'Raw' bands, Sentinel-2 SAFE, Landsat Collection 2, multiband
    stacks, Sentinel-1 VV/VH. Water from NDWI + confident MNDWI (or SAR)
    with an edge-based Otsu threshold; implausible thresholds are refused,
    not used. Permanent water from a pre-event image, ESA WorldCover or JRC
    occurrence. Flood-trace mode maps vegetation lost between two dates for
    floods that drained before the overpass.

    DEMs: FABDEM, Copernicus GLO-30, ALOS, TanDEM-X, the Google GEM 15 m DTM
    or LiDAR, as one file or as tiles (only the cells under the area are
    read). A Prepare DEM tool merges, clips and reprojects tiles with
    bilinear resampling and checks them for resampling artefacts.

    Depth: the water surface from the terrain along the flood edge (FwDET
    boundary method), or from a HAND raster. Uncertainty: a Monte Carlo
    ensemble with correlated DEM error and extent perturbation gives depth
    P10 / P50 / P90 and flood probability. Several image dates give the
    maximum observed depth and the dates flooded. Terrain-based estimates,
    not a hydraulic model.
