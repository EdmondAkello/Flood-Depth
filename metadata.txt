about=FloodDepth turns a satellite image of a flood (or a flood-extent layer)
    and a DEM into a flood-depth map with uncertainty bands, in one click.
    Every computation runs inside QGIS with NumPy and the GDAL bindings that
    QGIS ships; it never starts another process and needs no pip install.

    Imagery: select the downloaded files as they are - Copernicus / EO
    Browser 'Raw' bands, Sentinel-2 SAFE, Landsat Collection 2, multiband
    stacks, the Browser 'False color' product, Sentinel-1 VV/VH. Bands are
    recognised from file names and metadata. MNDWI, NDWI or SAR backscatter
    with a tile-based Otsu threshold and a separability score; implausible
    thresholds are refused, not used. Permanent water from a pre-event image,
    ESA WorldCover or JRC occurrence. Flood-trace mode maps vegetation lost
    between two dates for floods that drained before the overpass, and
    separates inundation from erosion / deposition evidence.

    Depth: the water surface from the terrain along the flood edge (FwDET
    boundary method) with edges at NoData, cloud or permanent water
    excluded, outlier screening and slope-aware smoothing; or from a HAND
    raster; or from a known surface or level.

    Uncertainty: a Monte Carlo ensemble with spatially correlated DEM error,
    threshold and edge perturbation gives depth P10 / P50 / P90 and flood
    probability; an evidence layer says what each flooded cell is based on.
    Every run writes an HTML report with the decisions, statistics, warnings
    and limitations. Terrain-based estimates, not a hydraulic model.
